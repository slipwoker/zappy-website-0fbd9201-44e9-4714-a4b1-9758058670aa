Our story

Creating with intention,
Designing with love

DP STUDIO was born from a deep passion for handcraft and home design. Every item we create — whether a shiny resin tray or a minimalist concrete element — is the result of a slow and intentional process, where each stage is performed with care and thought. We believe your home deserves objects that tell a story.

Our decorative items are made in Israel, in a unique combination of Resin and Concrete, giving each piece texture, depth and a finish that cannot be replicated. Every product is one of a kind — there is no other exactly like it in the world. This is not just decoration; this is the experience of owning something real and authentic.

Handcraft meets modern minimalism

The Philosophy of Quiet Beauty

At DP STUDIO we believe that true beauty lies in the small details — in texture that invites touch, in color born from the material itself, in form that feels both modern and timeless. Every item we create carries within it the time, intention and love invested in it.

The unique combination of resin and concrete allows us to work with contrasts: hardness and softness, transparency and depth, industrialism and elegance. The result is home decor items that feel personal and special — not because they are expensive, but because they are real. Every handmade resin tray, every bowl, every decorative item — is a conversation between the material and the hands that created it.

We believe in "slow" — in intentional creation, in finishing that takes time, in patience that leads to results that last for years. Every item is made in Israel, in our studio, one by one — because otherwise it simply wouldn't be what it is.


Each piece carries a subtle variation that is part of its story — not a flaw, but a signature. This is what makes every piece one of a kind.

Our materials

The art that begins with raw material

Every item at DP STUDIO is born from two unique materials — resin and concrete — which when they meet, create a stunning contrast between softness and strength, between shine and texture.

All Products

Resin — a dazzling shine

Resin is the liquid soul of every item. In its fluidity it is liquid, transparent and soft — and when it hardens it becomes a deep and shiny layer that reflects light like a gemstone. We work with high-grade quality resin, and add pigments, nuances and marble effects by hand. Each casting is a unique gesture — it can never be reproduced exactly.

Concrete — an anchor of substance

Concrete brings weight, texture and earthiness. It is rough, matte, and full of character — but in the right hands it becomes a delicate and precise sculpture. We mix and prepare each concrete mixture ourselves, and adjust the granularity, color and weight for each product separately. Our concrete is not industrial — it is intimate, intentional and calculated.

The harmony — when opposites meet

The real magic happens the moment resin and concrete meet. Shine versus matte, softness versus roughness, transparency versus opacity — all these create a design language that cannot be imitated. In every tray and decorative item from DP STUDIO, the two materials connect to form a complete creation: polished, unique, and with a presence that speaks for itself.

This is how every item is born

Every DP STUDIO creation undergoes a meticulous handwork process — from the casting stage to the final finish. The slight difference between item to item is not a flaw, but evidence of the authenticity of each creation.

01

Casting and painting

Every item begins with a precise mixture of resin and concrete, prepared by hand and poured into unique molds. At this stage, pigments, textures and decorative elements are added that create the unreplicable character of each creation.

02

Drying and hand finishing

After the casting stage, the item undergoes a controlled drying process. At the end of drying, each item is polished, smoothed and receives precise hand finishing — protective layers, texture polishing and treatment of small details that make each creation luxurious and ready for the home.

03

Final quality check

Before the item leaves the studio, it undergoes a personal and meticulous quality check. We ensure that every finish, every edge and every detail meets DP STUDIO's high standard — only then is the item ready to find its home.

Slight changes in shade, texture and pattern between item to item are an integral part of the character of true handwork — evidence that this is a unique and authentic creation, not mass production.
