Our Process

Handcraft That Begins
with Intention

At DP STUDIO, every piece is born from an intentional combination of resin and concrete — two materials that communicate with each other in creating rich, solid and elegant texture. The choice of materials is not accidental: it is the essence of our identity, and the foundation for every handcrafted decorative piece that leaves the studio.

01

Mixing and Casting

The resin and concrete are mixed in precise ratios, and cast into molds by hand. Each layer is poured carefully to create unique depth and texture that cannot be replicated.

02

Hand Finishing

After the material stabilizes, each piece undergoes manual polishing and shaping — a stage where the final character of the piece is revealed. The finishing is done in layers, with full attention to every detail.

03

Quality Control

Each piece is examined before it leaves the studio — touch, appearance, and weight. Only pieces that meet the highest standard are sent out.

The natural variation between piece to piece is not a flaw — it is the soul of authentic handwork.
