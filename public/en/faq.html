Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you wanted to know about DP STUDIO, our items and the ordering process

Is each item one of a kind?

Yes — this is one of the things closest to our hearts at DP STUDIO. Every item is handcrafted, and after the resin and concrete merge, a pattern is created that will never repeat itself. Shade, texture, veins and delicate details — all of these make each item completely unique. You receive not just a decorative object, but a small work of art that is incomparable.

Do you ship nationwide?

Absolutely. We ship throughout Israel. Every item is carefully and lovingly packed so it arrives to you intact and perfect. Shipping time may vary depending on the area and volume, but we make every effort to ship within several business days from when the item is ready. You can contact us for exact details about costs and shipping options.

Can I order an item as a gift?

Of course, and we really love it. DP STUDIO items are special gifts for occasions like birthdays, weddings, housewarming, anniversaries and any other joyful event. You can request special gift wrapping and even add a personal blessing. Just mention this when contacting us and we'll take care of all the details.

How long does it take to prepare an item?

Since every item is handcrafted and goes through several stages of production and processing — including drying and finishing time — the average preparation time is between 5 to 10 business days. Special or custom items may require additional time. We'll always update you throughout the process so you know when your item is on its way to you.

Can I order an item in a custom color or design?

We're happy to hear ideas and try to adapt the item to the atmosphere and colors of your home. Contact us with your idea — shade, size, or style — and we'll get back to you with options and a customized price. Every such collaboration is an opportunity to create something that didn't exist before.

How do you care for polymer concrete items?

Our items are durable and designed for daily use, but like any quality object — they come to caring hands. It's recommended to wipe with a damp cloth only, avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight, and not to soak in water. Handmade resin and concrete trays will maintain their beauty for years when properly cared for.

I have an event, what should I order?

If you're looking for a special and impressive gift for an event, we recommend our sets that include a tray and double candle holder - a perfect combination of luxurious design and functionality.
The sets are especially suitable for challah separation, housewarming, Shabbats, holidays and meaningful personal gifts. Every item is handcrafted, so each gift is truly unique and moving.

What to expect from DP STUDIO

Every item that leaves our studio is created by hand, with intention and love for materials. Here are some things that are important to know before ordering.

Natural variation between items

Every tray and decorative item is produced separately, so there may be slight differences in color, texture and finish between item to item. No two items are completely identical — this is exactly the soul of true handwork. The small differences are part of the uniqueness, not a flaw.

Careful and thoughtful packaging

Every item is carefully wrapped and ready for shipping or personal delivery. We ensure that every product arrives to you intact and beautifully presented — whether for personal use or as a gift. Our items, like concrete and resin decorative pieces, arrive ready for gifting.

Direct and Personal Communication

Have a question? Want to order a special gift and specify details like name, date or special request? We're here. At DP STUDIO we believe in personal connection with every customer — you can contact us at any time and we'll be happy to adapt the item to your needs.

Didn't find the answer you were looking for?

We're here for you. Any question, special request or just a desire to hear more about our items — we'd love to hear from you and respond personally.
